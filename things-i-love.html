For Loopy

January 2025

In a world of chaos, you bring peace,

A gentle soul that makes worries cease.

Your smile, a light in darkest night,

Makes everything feel just right.


Loyal heart that never strays,

Brightening even the gloomiest days.

Full of love, so pure and true,

This is my simple poem for you.

Soft Heart

January 2025

They call you a crybaby, but I see more,

A heart so tender, feelings to the core.

You feel deeply, you love with might,

Your soft heart makes the world more bright.


Don't hide those tears, they're part of you,

They show you're brave, honest, and true.

In a world that's hard, you stay soft still,

That's your strength, that's your will.

Pink and Red

January 2025

You love pink like cherry blossoms in spring,

And red like the passion that love can bring.

These colors suit you, soft yet bold,

A story of warmth that needs to be told.


Loopy and Snoopy by your side,

In your heart, they always reside.

Simple joys that make you smile,

You make life beautiful, worthwhile.
